# Marginalia

*A Monochrome fic (Weiss Schnee / Blake Belladonna). RWBY. Slow burn; hurt/comfort.*

Canon-adjacent AU. It follows the first year at Beacon, the Fall, the separation, and a winter in the north, and diverges from canon after Volume 3. Spoilers through Volume 3, and nothing past it that you would need to know.

**Rating:** Teen. **Content notes:** grief; the aftermath of an abusive relationship (nothing shown directly); a killing (off the page); institutional cruelty and bigotry; alcohol; a panic response.

Thirty-three chapters. The prose is plain on purpose; most of what happens is in what is left out.

Other editions, both generated from the chapter files: [index.html](index.html) is a single-page version set in the same typography as the site's front page (light and dark, readable on a phone); [marginalia-complete.md](marginalia-complete.md) is the whole text as one Markdown file.
