# 33. carried forward

**Day 150**

> Calm. +1. Four letters written. Council, Winter, Ruby, Mother. Each read twice. Return address: Camp Four, Hollin Gap. —W.S.

> Concur. —B.B.

**Day 151**

> NW. +2. T.D. took the letters down on the sled. Said, "Neat hand." Said, "You've put the address." Said, "It matters. You've only decided to let it." —W.S.

> She's right. —B.B.

> I know. —W.S.

**Day 153**

> SW. +4. River open at the bend. Grayling, two. —B.B.

> Of course it's good. —W.S.

**Day 154**

> Rain. +5. South roof leaks. Buckets: six. —W.S.

**Day 155**

> Rain. Roof patched. Dispute re sealant. —B.B.

> I was right. —W.S.

> You were. —B.B.

> I should like that noted. —W.S.

> Noted. —B.B.

**Day 156**

> Overcast. +3. Dog. Not mine. —W.S.

**Day 157**

> Overcast. Dog on cot. Removed. Dog on cot. —B.B.

> This is not a decision. —W.S.

**Day 158**

> Clear. +6. Staying? —W.S.

> Today. —B.B.

> You? —B.B.

> Today. —W.S.

**Day 160**

> Clear. +7. Ptarmigan on the scree, going brown at the edges. Eleven, and one that was lame. Counted him separately. —B.B.

> Birds are not witnesses. —W.S.

> Then who are you counting for. —B.B.

> Whoever reads it. —W.S.

**Day 161**

> Brand at the foot. Mail. Ruby, Yang, Klein (bread stale), Winter. The name is Maren Holt. It was glass. —W.S.

> Ask T.D. about Lamp Street. —B.B.

> Tomorrow. —W.S.

**Day 162**

> Roads open. Wind S. +8. Departure: Tuesday. —W.S.

> Wednesday. —B.B.

> Argued. —W.S.

> Thursday, after the fish. —B.B.

> Agreed. —W.S.

**Day 163**

> Rain. +6. T.D.: Lamp Street, a Holt, a laundress, "she'd sing to the ones who couldn't sleep." Knew two people who knew her. Will ask. Does not say dead. Does not say not. —W.S.

> Come up. —B.B.

> Yes. —W.S.

**Day 164**

> Calm. +7. Letter for the Council recopied, in case. Copy of the log made; original stays. Read it. Twice. —W.S.

> I read it three times. —B.B.

**Day 165**

> Brand says dogs do not go on trains. —B.B.

> Brand says dogs go on trains if they are wearing a collar. A collar has been procured. It is red. —W.S.

> It is not red. It is orange. —B.B.

> It is red. —W.S.

> It was the only one. —B.B.

**Day 166**

> Calm. +5. Thursday. Fish eaten. Departed 0900. Key with T.D. Lamp on the sill, trimmed and filled. Original log left in the desk drawer, string tied, pencil on the string. Copy taken. Book taken. Rules sheet taken. —W.S.

> Dog: taken. —B.B.

> We are not keeping the dog. —W.S.

> We are keeping the dog. —B.B.

> Yes. —W.S.

> The stove is banked. The door is not locked. —B.B.

> Staying? —W.S.

> Today. —B.B.

> Page full. Carried forward. —W.S.

> Again. —B.B.
